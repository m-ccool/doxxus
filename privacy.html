Privacy policy

What I collect when you contact me or sign in, why, and who else handles it.

9 sections
  1. Who this coversThis policy covers doxxus.us, run by B McCool. It describes what is collected when you send a message, request a package, or sign in to the client portal, and what happens to it.
  2. Messages and package requestsWhen you use the contact form or the package builder, your name, email address, phone number, the type of request and your message are sent to a database I control (Supabase) and emailed to me from my own mailbox. You also get an automatic email confirming receipt. I use this only to reply to you and to scope the work you asked about.
  3. Client sign-inThe client portal uses Sign in with Google, provided through Supabase Authentication. The portal also supports signing in with a one-time code sent to your email address. Either way I receive your email address (and, with Google, your name), and use it only to match you to your client record and show you your own details and projects. I do not access your Google contacts, files or any other Google data, and I do not ask for it.
  4. Project informationFor clients I store a name, email, phone, business or project name, project status and links, and a meeting link. I do not store card numbers, passwords or files in the portal.
  5. PaymentsPayments are processed by Stripe. Card and bank details go to Stripe and are not seen or stored by me. I keep only invoice and payment references.
  6. Third partiesData is handled by Supabase (database, sign-in and functions), Namecheap (my email provider), Google (sign-in) and Stripe (payments). The site also loads fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs, which can see your IP address when your browser fetches them. I do not sell your information and I do not run advertising trackers.
  7. Browser storageWhen you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. The site does not use advertising cookies.
  8. Keeping and deleting dataI keep messages and client records for as long as I need them to respond to you and to run a project. You can ask me to delete or correct your information at any time by emailing dev@doxxus.us.
  9. Changes and contactIf this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: dev@doxxus.us.
